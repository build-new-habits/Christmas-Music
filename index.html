Christmas Tunes

This app shows each tune from New Christmas Praise (Solo & 1st Cornet B♭) on its own, with its tempo and a tempo light.

The book isn’t included in the app. Choose your PDF copy once on each device. The app cuts out every tune and keeps them only on this device, so it then works without the internet.

Using it on a phone or tablet

Add the app to your home screen so it opens full screen and your stored music is kept. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the Install app button when it appears, or open the browser menu (⋮) and tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
